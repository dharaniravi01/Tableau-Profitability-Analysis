# Tableau Profitability & Discount Strategy Analysis

## Project Overview

This project uses Tableau to analyse global sales and profitability data, with a deeper investigation into the financial performance of the Nigerian market.

The analysis identified Nigeria as a major loss-making market, with a **-148.57% profit margin**. Further analysis was conducted to investigate the impact of discounting, product performance, customer segments and shipping costs.

Five Tableau dashboards were developed to identify the key drivers of losses and evaluate potential strategies for improving profitability.

## Tools & Skills

- Tableau
- Data Visualisation
- Business Analytics
- Data Analysis
- Scenario Analysis
- KPI Analysis

## Dashboards

### 1. Overall Profitability Overview

Analysed sales and profitability across markets, customer segments and product categories.

Key finding:
- Nigeria recorded a **-148.57% profit margin**, compared with positive margins in markets such as China and India.

### 2. Profitability Analysis – Nigeria

Investigated profitability across Nigerian states, customer segments and product sub-categories.

Key findings:
- Lagos recorded the largest loss at **-$25,922.51**.
- All major customer segments operated at a loss.
- Paper, Storage and Machines were among the sub-categories with the lowest profit margins.

### 3. Sales & Discount Analysis

Analysed the relationship between product sales and discounting in Nigeria.

Key findings:
- A uniform **70% discount** was applied across Furniture, Office Supplies and Technology.
- Office Supplies recorded the highest sales volume.
- Despite continued sales, all major product categories remained unprofitable.

### 4. Shipping Analysis

Evaluated shipping costs, shipping methods and customer order priorities.

Key findings:
- Standard Class had the lowest average shipping cost at **$19.98** and was the most frequently used shipping method.
- Same Day and First Class had substantially higher average shipping costs.
- Most orders were classified as Medium priority, suggesting that customers generally did not require premium delivery.

### 5. Discount Scenario Analysis

Evaluated the potential financial impact of reducing Nigeria's discount rate from **70% to 50%**.

The scenario analysis showed that reducing the discount could substantially decrease projected losses, although profitability would remain negative.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, a phased discount reduction strategy was proposed:

- Reduce discounts gradually by **5 percentage points per quarter** until reaching 50%.
- Monitor sales volume and profit margins after each reduction.
- Replace uniform discounting with more targeted promotions.
- Introduce loyalty incentives to help retain price-sensitive customers.
- Simplify shipping options and explore lower-cost logistics solutions.

## Project Outcome

The analysis demonstrated how Tableau dashboards can be used to move from identifying a business problem to investigating its underlying drivers and developing data-driven recommendations.

The findings suggest that excessive discounting was a major contributor to Nigeria's losses, while shipping costs were a secondary factor. A phased pricing strategy was therefore recommended to reduce losses while managing the risk of customer attrition.
